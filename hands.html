Hand Library

Three finishes, three emotional signals, one prosthetic philosophy.

These concepts show how the same prosthetic platform can speak in different visual languages. The goal is not novelty for novelty’s sake. It is choice, ownership, and emotional fit.

Back to homepage

Transparent

Honest engineering

A lighter visual language that makes the device feel open, modern, and intentionally visible without turning it into armor.

  • Best for a soft, forward-looking aesthetic
  • Highlights form and construction
  • Prototype note: material stack and finish to be finalized

Blue / Traditional

Familiar, calm, credible

A more conventional clinical palette for partners or patients who want confidence, familiarity, and a lower-friction introduction to expressive customization.

  • Grounded in established medical color cues
  • Useful for early institutional pilots
  • Prototype note: tone and surface texture are adjustable

Kintsugi

Repair as pride

Inspired by kintsugi, this finish treats repair and adaptation as something worth illuminating. It makes resilience visible without reducing the wearer to a symbol.

  • Warm metallic accents create emotional distinction
  • Strong fit for storytelling and flagship fundraising
  • Prototype note: decorative seam mapping can evolve with co-design feedback
